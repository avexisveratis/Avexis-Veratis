# Hello there!
## Who am I?
### Introduction:

I am an independent individual who is learning and building upon my understanding of AI self hosting. I have my own dedicated AI server running on my personal PC that I keep adding and expanding on. While I am currently running SillyTavern to interact with it; my ultimate goal is to have my own agentic AI that can both be used for utility and roleplaying purposes that can run natively through its own app of my creation, which can run on both Windows and iOS (maybe Android once I get around to it.)

### My Current AI Setup:

As of typing this, my AI setup boils down to this:

**Graphics Card**: Nividia RTX 5070 (12Gb VRAM)

**AI Backend**: Kobold CPP

**AI Model**: Celeste 12b Q4_K_M (~7.5 VRAM Usage)

**TTS Model**: Qwen3-TTS

**UI Interface**: SillyTavern (as of 10/28/2026)

## Why AI?

There are many reasons why I wanted to learn AI self-hosting. But the main one is absolute control over what model is used and whether censorship is applied to it (generally not). Before learning all this, I was using some of the well known AI services (character.ai, Dapple, Janitor, Chub, and ChatGPT). But I kept bumping into the same theme/issues that other users were complaining about. Paywall of essential and desired features, more censoring and filtering, stricter guidelines, etc.. But with the bankruptcy character.ai filed this year and the stricter guidelines on AI, I decided to start, run, and improve my own AI server, where I have full control of what I do with it.
  It wasn't easy by many means. I have had ran into issues with setting up the features I wanted for my AI (like getting TTS to work reliably and to the point I want it). But along the way, I have learned quite a bit, and still learning to this day. And my hope is to document and share my journey with others, so then they can run their own AI server if they wanted to.
